=== ORPHANED VALIDATED INVOICES INVESTIGATION ===

1. Root Cause Mechanism:
   - services/api/internal/submit/submit.go:
     queue: make(chan string, 100000)
     Enqueue(ids ...string) -> s.queue <- id
   - An upload validates invoices and sets status to 'Validated' in PostgreSQL.
   - Invoices are sent into the in-memory Go channel `s.queue`.
   - When the container is OOM-killed, all contents of `s.queue` are erased from memory.
   - On container restart, `main.go` initializes a brand new `Submitter` and starts 4 workers.
   - BUT `main.go` and `submitter.Start` DO NOT query PostgreSQL for pending 'Validated' invoices!
   - Result: Invoices validated prior to the crash remain in 'Validated' status in the database forever, never picked up by submit workers.

2. Database Query:
SELECT v.id, v.invoice_number, s.name AS seller_name, v.status, v.created_at, v.updated_at
FROM invoices v
JOIN sellers s ON s.id = v.seller_id
WHERE v.status = 'Validated' AND v.updated_at < '2026-11-16 09:42:00+04'
ORDER BY v.updated_at ASC;

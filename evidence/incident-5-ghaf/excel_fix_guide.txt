=== CUSTOMER STEP-BY-STEP EXCEL GUIDE: FIXING DATE FORMAT ===

Why this happens:
The UAE Federal Tax Authority (FTA) Peppol e-invoicing standard requires dates in the international standard format: YYYY-MM-DD (Year-Month-Day).
When dates are typed as text like "05/11/2026", computers cannot reliably tell if it means 5th November or 11th May. To prevent tax compliance errors, the portal requires YYYY-MM-DD.

How to fix your file in 60 seconds:

Method 1: Change to YYYY-MM-DD
1. In your Excel file, click on the "Invoice issue date*" column (Column D).
2. Replace:
   - 13/11/2026 with 2026-11-13
   - 14/11/2026 with 2026-11-14
   - 15/11/2026 with 2026-11-15
3. In the "Payment due date*" column (Column E), replace:
   - 13/12/2026 with 2026-12-13
   - 14/12/2026 with 2026-12-14
   - 15/12/2026 with 2026-12-15
4. Save the file and upload again.

Method 2: Use Excel Date Format
1. Highlight columns D and E.
2. Right-click and choose "Format Cells..." (or press Ctrl+1).
3. Under "Category", select "Date".
4. Choose the format that looks like 2012-03-14 (YYYY-MM-DD).
5. Click OK, save, and upload.
